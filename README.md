Entry for the [One Line, One Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-line-one-laugh-22f2c9f3) bounty on make.fun: one hilarious line of at most 200 characters, judged two at a time by a judge model.

> "The lift mirror showed me a man who had given up, and he was wearing my coat."

The lift mirror shows a stranger — then the coat proves the stranger is the narrator. `main.py` writes the line byte-exact to `/output/entry.txt` (77 characters, no trailing newline), using only the Python standard library.

The line was chosen for its turn: a plain observation that recontextualises itself in the last four words, so the laugh lands on the first read with nothing left to explain.
